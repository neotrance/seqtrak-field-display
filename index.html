AN INDEPENDENT VISUAL COMPANION

Keep your hands on the instrument.

Made for sound design, sequencing, and live performance, SEQTRAK Field Display brings sound, sequence, and performance state into quick view as you create or play live. The original instrument remains at the center, represented by a monochrome schematic alongside a 320 × 170 pixel preview of the external display.

EXPLORE THE SIMULATOR

Drag or scroll the FX knob to cycle the documented 8-category effect presets. Click MASTER/SINGLE to change the target and use the simulator FX Page control below the hardware drawing to switch parameter assignments. Drag a touch strip to adjust its assigned value. On Page 1, HIGH PASS and REPEATER stay active only while pressed and bypass on release. Click the 16 Drum-section pads to toggle preview steps; they update the display and step grid below. These controls are simulations only and produce no audio or hardware connection. Drum-section pads are sequence controls; Synth-section keys perform notes. The sounding Synth note depends on the selected track and its Scale, Key, and Octave settings, so this page's C4–B4 buttons simulate MIDI note events and do not claim a physical-key mapping. REC and note controls are simulations only: this page does not connect to SEQTRAK or detect recording state. DrumKit uses a neutral symbol when its active sound is unknown.
