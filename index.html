OPERATION NOTES

Drag or scroll the FX knob to cycle the documented 8-category effect presets. Hold a track knob while rotating FX to step through that track's sound presets; the selected sound name appears on the external display and in the sound preset control below. Click MASTER/SINGLE to change the effect target and use the simulator FX Page control below the hardware drawing to switch parameter assignments. Drag a touch strip to adjust its assigned value. On Page 1, HIGH PASS and REPEATER stay active only while pressed and bypass on release. Click the 16 Drum-section pads to toggle preview steps; they update the display and step grid below. Hold ALL while pressing Drum keys 1–6 to queue the same pattern for all 11 tracks; double-tap a Drum key while holding ALL to switch immediately. These hardware controls remain simulations and produce no audio. When you connect a MIDI input, this page receives messages only: documented CC values and Note activity can update the display, while Program Change and unknown SysEx are logged without guessed sound names or unverified decoding. Drum-section pads are sequence controls; Synth-section keys perform notes. The sounding Synth note depends on the selected track and its Scale, Key, and Octave settings, so this page's C4–B4 buttons simulate note events and do not claim a physical-key mapping. Recording, saved patterns, and unconfirmed hardware states are not inferred. DrumKit uses a neutral symbol when its active sound is unknown.
